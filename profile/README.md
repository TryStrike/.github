Strike is a binary options app on Arc. call higher or lower on crypto, forex, stocks and metals in rounds of 5 seconds to 1 minute, settled in USDC or EURC

you deposit once and play from a house balance, so a round needs no wallet popup.. and every part of a round can be checked without trusting us

- **Arc**: USDC in and out of the StrikeTreasury contract, gas paid in USDC, a Merkle root of every settled round committed every 10 minutes
- **Circle**: Gateway and CCTP bring USDC from Base, Arbitrum or Ethereum Sepolia, and send it back out
- **Arkiv**: every settled round and every bet is a public entity anyone can query
- **Swarm**: the full proof bundle for each epoch, stamped with our own postage batch
- **World ID**: one verified human behind every wallet and every agent, with Agent Pass approvals for the risky moves
